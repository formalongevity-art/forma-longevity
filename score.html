See the research
  • VO₂ max · Mandsager et al., JAMA Network Open 2018. 122,007 adults; higher fitness, lower mortality, with no upper limit observed.
  • Grip strength · Leong et al., The Lancet 2015 (PURE). Almost 140,000 adults in 17 countries; each 5 kg lower grip linked to 16% higher all-cause mortality.
  • Resting heart rate · Zhang et al., CMAJ 2016 meta-analysis. Risk rises from 60 bpm and more steeply above 80 bpm.
  • Sleep · Cappuccio et al., Sleep 2010 meta-analysis. Both short and long sleep linked to higher mortality; the lowest risk sits around 7 to 8 hours.
  • Steps · Paluch et al., Lancet Public Health 2022. Benefit levels off around 8,000 to 10,000 steps under 60, and 6,000 to 8,000 from 60.
