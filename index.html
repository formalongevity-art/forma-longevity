The FORMA

FORMA turns what science already knows about living longer into something real, practiced, measured, and carried forward.

One week is the beginning, not the whole journey. FORMA is a community, not a calendar event. The people you train with in Koh Phangan are the same people you'll see again. FORMA is built for people who move regularly, not for competitive athletes. If you train two or three times a week, you're ready.

Throughout the year, we bring the community back together in Switzerland with smaller gatherings, so the habits built on the island don't stay on the island.

And everything is measured. Every guest completes a check up at the beginning, another at the end, and follow ups in the future, helping us build a clearer picture of what truly moves the needle on longevity.

Data driven, from day one

FORMA isn't just a week away. It's a longitudinal, data driven approach to living longer, built with the people doing it alongside you.

Koh Phangan & Arcana Wellness Village

Koh Phangan is a place to slow down, reset, and reconnect with how you want to live. It's less about the island itself and more about what happens when a group commits to the same days, side by side. Arcana Wellness Village sits inland, away from the noise.

The property is built for exactly this kind of week, with a spa, ice bath, pool, gym, sauna, restaurant, and daily in hotel activities, all a short walk from your room.

Longevity Testing

What gets measured, gets improved.

Every guest is tested at the start of the week, tested again before leaving, and checked in with 90 days later. A real before and after, not just a feeling.

HRV

Heart rate variability, a core marker of recovery and nervous system balance.

Heart Rate

Resting and active heart rate, tracked across the week.

Grip Strength

A simple, proven measure of overall strength and longevity.

Body Composition

Muscle and fat distribution, measured, not guessed.

Mobility & Balance

Functional range of motion and stability testing.

Blood Pressure

Cardiovascular baseline, checked at intake and exit.

Bring Your Wearables

Oura, Whoop, Garmin, Apple Watch, or similar. If you wear one, bring it, and we'll fold your data into the picture.

Everything above is rechecked before you leave, and again 90 days later.

Once your deposit is confirmed, we'll send you a short form with a few questions, including any health considerations we should know about.

Meet the Team

André

Founder & Coach

André moves fluidly between two worlds: the structured, evidence driven side of pharma and digital health, and the raw, physical world of sport. As a Senior Service Designer, he brings a human centered lens to health systems, and as a former professional football player and coach, he knows firsthand what it takes to push a body and a team to perform.

Today that duality shows up in how he coaches: leading outdoor HIIT classes in Zürich, staying sharp across tennis, mountain biking, skiing, ski touring, and diving, and bringing the same discipline that built his career into every session he runs.
