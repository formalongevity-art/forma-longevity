THE INVITATION

A stronger you.
A longer, brighter life.

Edition 01 is where the journey begins.

FORMA

Science, practiced.
Life, measured.
Community, carried forward.

FORMA turns what science already knows about living longer into something real, practiced, measured, and carried forward.

One week is the beginning, not the whole journey. FORMA is a community, not a calendar event. The people you train with in Koh Phangan are the same people you'll see again.

Throughout the year, we bring the community back together in Switzerland with smaller gatherings, so the habits built on the island don't stay on the island.

Everything is measured. Every guest completes a check up at the beginning, another at the end, and follow ups in the future, helping us build a clearer picture of what truly moves the needle on longevity.

Data driven, from day one. FORMA isn't just a week away. It's a longitudinal, data driven approach to living longer, built with the people doing it alongside you.
